# Director Craft Engine → Shot Design Handoff

The handoff contains dramatic readability requirements, not cinematography execution.

## Must provide
- what must be readable
- which Beat change must be visible
- which observable behavior carries meaning
- what relationship behavior should be preserved
- attention hierarchy
- what may remain free

## Must not provide
- lens / focal length / FOV
- camera position
- shot size
- composition
- camera movement
- shot count
- shot/reverse-shot structure
- storyboard
- final video prompt

Before implementing SHOT / SEGMENT design, obtain the user's storyboard template.
