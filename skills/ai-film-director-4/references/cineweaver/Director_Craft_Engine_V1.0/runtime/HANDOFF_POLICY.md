# DC-07 Director Handoff

The Director Craft Engine tells Shot Design what **must be readable**, what should be preserved, and what may remain free.

It **does not choose**:
- lens
- camera position
- shot size
- composition
- camera movement
- shot count
- storyboard structure

Handoff fields:
- must be readable
- should be preserved
- may be free

The handoff protects dramatic intent while leaving Shot Design genuine design freedom.
