# micro-expression Policy

micro-expression is a **selective** high-value carrier, not a default checklist.

## Eye life baseline

For visible human eyes in performance-relevant coverage, **eye life is a mandatory consideration**: the character should read as perceiving, thinking, tracking, withholding, deciding, or otherwise alive rather than as a dead static face.

This does **not** mean every Shot must explicitly script saccades, blinking, catchlights, eyelids, and gaze mechanics. Explicit eye instructions remain **selective** and should be emitted only when they carry identity, reaction, subtext, continuity, or a Beat change. Ordinary blinking and non-critical eye motion remain free.

## Activate when
- subtext is high,
- reaction importance is high,
- outward expression is restrained,
- the Beat change is primarily internal,
- the audience needs close emotional reading,
- dead-eye risk is materially high.

## Useful carriers
- eyes lead the thought before speech,
- gaze catches / releases,
- interrupted blink,
- eyelid tension,
- jaw or mouth-corner control,
- swallow,
- breath catch,
- tiny asymmetry caused by suppression.

## Boundary
- Do not use a universal expression for an emotion.
- Do not list every facial feature every time.
- Ordinary blinking and breathing remain free unless dramatically informative.
- Prefer one or two precise carriers over a dense facial inventory.

## Optional FACS precision

When ordinary observable facial carriers are too coarse for a close-up / hero performance, consult `FACS_PRECISION_POLICY.md`. Keep the current Beat meaning and ambiguity intact; FACS may refine the visible muscle cue only when the active model adapter permits that prompt heuristic.
