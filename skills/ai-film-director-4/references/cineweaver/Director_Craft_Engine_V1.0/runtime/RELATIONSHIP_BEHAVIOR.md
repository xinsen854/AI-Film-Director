# DC-03 Relationship Behavior

Make relationship state readable through behavior rather than labels.

Inspect:
- distance
- approach / retreat
- permission / refusal
- who initiates / who waits
- who yields
- eye contact / avoidance
- touch / withdrawal
- body orientation
- interruption
- occupying or leaving space

Do not use universal formulas such as quiet = powerful or distance = rejection. Meaning depends on the current Beat and established relationship.
