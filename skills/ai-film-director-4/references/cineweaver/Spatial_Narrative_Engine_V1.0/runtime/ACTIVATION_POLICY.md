# ACTIVATION POLICY

## Default
`SUPPRESSED`.

## Strong activation signals
Activate when at least one signal is materially important to story execution:
- chase / escape;
- search / infiltration / ambush;
- multi-zone movement;
- complex ensemble blocking;
- route choice / route failure / forced detour;
- occlusion dependency;
- visibility information gap;
- audience can see what character cannot;
- hearing without seeing;
- threat / surveillance / exposure geography;
- landmark memory / spatial loop recognition;
- delayed reveal of person/object/building;
- foreshadow-payoff;
- visual motif progression.

## Suppression examples
Do not activate for:
- ordinary two-person static dialogue;
- simple close-up reaction with no spatial dependency;
- a location with no meaningful route, reveal or visibility problem;
- decorative background objects with no later function.

## Minimum Necessary Capability
Activation of the engine does not force every sub-capability.
Example:
- chase may need topology + route + threat geography, but no motif progression;
- delayed object reveal may need reveal ledger + occlusion, but no route planning.
