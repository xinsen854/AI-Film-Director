# Capability Router V1.1 — Conflict Rules

Router identifies conflicts between capability intentions. It does not resolve them by inventing shot design.

## RESTRAINT vs EXTERNALIZED_PERFORMANCE
Use Scene Intelligence emotional mechanism + energy profile to establish which is dominant. If evidence is mixed, preserve the conflict and route both as a downstream method question rather than forcing one globally.

## STATIC_OBSERVATION vs HIGH_CAMERA_INTERVENTION
This is a Director Craft conflict. Router records the conflict and forwards the scene evidence. Router does not choose camera movement.

## FREE vs HARD on the same variable
The same variable cannot be both FREE and HARD. HARD wins only when a verified story/identity/continuity/spatial/physics/dialogue/action-result risk exists. Otherwise downgrade to SOFT or FREE.

## PHYSICAL_BUSINESS vs STILLNESS
These are not globally exclusive. They may be locally combined: one character can remain still while another uses physical business. Router should avoid applying either behavior to the whole scene without evidence.

## Conflict output
Record:
- capabilities involved
- source evidence
- whether the conflict is resolved at Router level or deferred
- downstream owner
