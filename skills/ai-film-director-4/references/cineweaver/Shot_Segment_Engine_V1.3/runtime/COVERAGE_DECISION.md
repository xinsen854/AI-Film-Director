# COVERAGE DECISION

Choose coverage after Director Craft states what must be readable.

May decide:
- shot size,
- camera position,
- angle,
- camera behavior,
- composition priority,
- depth strategy,
- perspective/compression tendency,
- subject prominence.

Decision tests:
1. Can the primary readable event be seen clearly?
2. Can the required relationship/spatial information be read?
3. Is reaction more important than speaker/action source?
4. Does camera intervention help or distract?
5. Is the coverage executable in one continuous generation?

No fixed mappings from scene type to lens/shot size.


## Whole-sequence Monotony Audit｜非阻断
After coverage is drafted, read only the explicit **shot size / 景别** and **camera move / 摄影机运动** fields as one sequence column. **Three consecutive** SHs with the same shot size **and** the same camera move trigger a `WARNING` for mechanical repetition.

The audit is advisory: same size alone or same move alone is legal; missing structured evidence is not guessed from prose; intentional formal repetition is allowed. Do not create extra cuts or change a successful dramatic setup solely to silence this warning.
