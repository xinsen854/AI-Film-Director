## Evidence levels

- **A:** authorized/public screenplay or verified draft + finished-film evidence + creator/guild interview where available.
- **B:** finished-film evidence + reliable scene/script excerpts or creator commentary; draft unavailable or uncertain.
- **C:** official recognition/selection record only; use for sampling and future research, not detailed craft claims.

Do not promote a case to A merely because a screenplay PDF exists. Verify title, draft type, provenance, and whether claims concern the page or the released film.

## Recognition archives

- [Writers Guild Awards](https://awards.wga.org/) — film, television, streaming, news, radio, and promotional writing.

## Screenplay access routes

Use these only for research. Do not redistribute or bundle script text.

- Studio/distributor FYC pages during awards season — highest preference when the PDF is released by the rights holder.
- [Deadline Read the Screenplay](https://deadline.com/story-arc/read-the-screenplay-series/) — awards-season pages often link to authorized campaign PDFs.
- [SimplyScripts awards-season tracker](https://simplyscripts.com/oscar-screenplays-98.html) — index to studio-posted FYC scripts; verify the final destination.
- [Script Slug](https://www.scriptslug.com/) — educational archive of produced screenplays; its own terms state fair-use educational access and no commercial rights.
- [BBC Writers script resources](https://www.bbc.co.uk/writers) — authorized television script and craft resources when available.
- Published screenplay books from rights holders, distributors, or established publishers.

## Expansion protocol

For every new case, record:

`title | year | country/region | language | format | genre | award/selection signal | script source | film evidence | creator source | evidence level | mechanism tags | cultural caveat | research status`

Balance each expansion batch across:

- region and language;
- era;
- genre and budget;
- original and adapted work;
- theatrical, television, animation, short and branded formats;
- tragic, comic, suspense, horror, romance, family, historical and experimental modes;
- winners, nominees, audience successes, and useful failures.

## Copyright and inference limits

- Store links and analytical abstractions, not full copyrighted screenplays.
- Do not quote more than necessary to identify a craft point.
- An accessible PDF is not a license for commercial reuse.
- An award proves recognition within a system, not universal quality.
- A screenplay draft may differ materially from the released film.
- A critic's reading is an interpretation; label it as such.
- Do not universalize a culturally specific family, class, religious, gender, or political behavior without local evidence.
