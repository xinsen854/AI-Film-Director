# Award-recognized corpus index

## How to use this index

This is a sampling and research queue, not a canon of universal quality. “Recognized” includes winners, screenplay winners, major nominees, jury prizes, guild selections, or works represented in reputable professional polls. Check `source-registry.md` before making a specific award claim.

Status tags:

- **CARD:** a mechanism card exists in the first release.
- **STUDY:** selected for screenplay/film/interview study.
- **INDEX:** broad comparison or future research; do not make detailed page claims without new evidence.

## Balance gaps for the next expansion

The current pool still overrepresents feature drama, East Asian/European festival cinema, and English-language guild recognition. Expand deliberately into African, Latin American, Middle Eastern, South/Southeast Asian, Indigenous, documentary-hybrid, children's television, comedy series, vertical short drama, and non-Western advertising traditions before treating any pattern as general.
